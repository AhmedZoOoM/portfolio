About

I'm a senior video editor with over a decade of experience in broadcast, commercial, and digital media production. My work centers on narrative-driven editing, color grading, and post-production workflow optimization.

I believe in the power of thoughtful editing—where every cut serves the story. Whether working on documentary features, broadcast commercials, or digital content, I approach each project with meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to visual excellence.

My technical proficiency spans industry-standard tools including DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Creative Suite, Avid Media Composer, and Final Cut Pro, with particular strength in color grading and editorial work.
